# Local recovery and deletion/export accounting

3 October 2026. Requirements SYS-002/M05, MEM-010, MEM-013 and RUN-001.

The existing job ledger remains the authority. An actual child coordinator was
killed with SIGKILL after acquiring a lease. After restarting from the same SQLite
database and expiring that lease, side-effect-free work requeued and finished once
under the original job ID (two attempts, one result). Work marked as possibly
effectful became `effect_unknown` and was not leased again. This exercises process
failure and the existing recovery policy, not a second node or an isolated worker.

Lifecycle improvements:

- A durable forget/revocation intent is replayed when the store opens, before reads
  or workers. Tests interrupt between journal fsync and database mutation.
- Journal appends handle partial writes, serialise appenders and fsync the parent.
  Corrupt/torn tails stop restore/startup; incomplete privacy intents are never dropped.
  Recovery needs a verified complete journal/backup, not deletion of the bad tail.
- Strict-policy transitions are journalled. Restoring a backup older than a grant
  revocation or migration cannot revive legacy source access. Newly issued grants
  absent from that backup must be reissued deliberately; restore does not invent them.
- Cancelled source/memory-derived action payloads are redacted. Original fingerprints
  remain historical metadata and are not approvals for the redacted placeholder.
  Completed drafts/effects remain explicitly outside the undo claim.
- Export publication rechecks credential/grant revision, statement versions/current
  support and executive versions under the transaction protecting publication.
  Revocation between rendering and publication leaves no readable export.
- A local export ledger records writing/published/failed/uncertain copies and their source/claim
  lineage. Forgetting marks known copies `retained_copy_review`; the copy is not
  silently deleted. Moved or externally duplicated copies cannot be located or recalled.
- Export intents are journalled before publication, so restoring a backup taken
  before that export preserves its copy/lineage accounting. Unsettled intents
  become `publication_unknown`; a failed receipt after rename also records
  uncertainty instead of implying that no readable copy exists. Conservative
  metadata is retained even if a pre-publication failure left no final copy.

`tests/test_memory_lifecycle.py`, `tests/test_export.py` and
`tests/test_pilot_recovery.py` passed 53 checks at this increment. The export race
test exercises the actual staged writer. Source removal continues to purge indexed
content, dependent answers and job artefacts/cache through the existing path.
Combined validation and exact commits are in the final stage receipt.

The inspectable `examples/systemd/alfred-core.service` is a user-service example,
not an installation or proof of unattended operation. Adapt the checkout/Python/data
paths, review custody and selected sources, then test start/stop, lock exclusion and
recovery before separately approving activation. It keeps the listener on loopback;
running services and child processes do not survive snapshots by assumption.

Remaining: application encryption/key custody, backup/export encryption and rotation,
externally copied artefact accounting, storage-media secure erasure, hardware/OS
service acceptance, remote transport and real second-node recovery. VACUUM, a clean
current table scan and journal replay do not erase prior WAL/backups/export/media.
No crypto library was adopted and no private data or service was deployed.
