# ALFRED: operational and executive intelligence

Owner direction confirmed 27 September 2026. This is the current product category, superseding narrower 'personal assistant' or operational-only shorthand without removing personal use.

**ALFRED is operational and executive intelligence delivered through a personal AI operating environment.** It maintains useful context, helps a person or authorised team understand what is happening, decide what matters, coordinate work and verify the outcome of deliberately authorised actions.

## Executive intelligence

Priorities, decision briefs, project/business context, planning, people and responsibilities, commitments, preparation and follow-up. The target is continuity from information to judgement to delegated work, not simply another dashboard showing disconnected records.

## Operational intelligence

Current state and source health, meaningful changes, coordination, authorised system/device information, task execution and honest receipts. Reports, observed telemetry, simulation, analysis, human acknowledgement and completed effects remain different evidence types.

## Personal operating environment

The user remains central. Personal knowledge, conversation, preferences, everyday administration and devices are not removed by the executive category. Personal, company and each client/operation need separate permissions, retention and data-egress choices. The same coherent interface is not a universal shared memory pool.

The premium black/graphite/ivory, sparse-amber console is the interaction direction. The Obsidian note application is an optional editor over user-owned Markdown, not ALFRED's brand or mandatory user interface. The console's internal Obsidian codename refers to its visual direction, not a completed integration with that editor.

## Requirements and present state

The existing 27 PRD requirements and 12 memory jobs continue. Executive outcomes are carried through INT-001/002, ATT-001, MEM-004/006/015 and ACT-001; operational outcomes through OPS-001, CON-001, ACT-001, RUN-001 and MEM-003/009/010. UX-001 must support both without turning fixture activity into apparent real telemetry.

The source is consolidated, not the finished end-state product. The real backend and newer fixture console still need their authenticated adapter. Reviewed-memory retrieval, reliable production reasoning, account/device connectors and voice are unfinished. Merging or copying code does not implement those missing capabilities.

Emotive Impact versus Black State corporate ownership remains unresolved. ENDSTATE and Noir remain independent specialist systems until their actual interfaces are reviewed and adapters implemented. No autonomous use of force, covert capture or safety-critical operational readiness is granted or claimed by this positioning.
